Program Overview

About the Program

The competition is organized with DLAB Consulting and Gies College of Business. You trade in IBKR's paper environment with the same starting capital as every other team, document your reasoning, and are evaluated on both results and how clearly you explain your strategy.

Program Highlights

  • Real-market workflow without real-money risk — paper accounts only.
  • Governance checkpoints: Rule Freeze before launch, Tech Green-Light (48 hrs pre-launch), and a mid-competition health review at end of Week 2.
  • Aligned with university academic integrity and zero tolerance for insider trading.
  • Document version 2.0 (Spring 2026) — draft, pending approval. Prepared by Grace, Daniel, Pranav, Amar, Villy, Paul, Elizabeth, and Nathan.

Official Rules Charter

Rules & Eligibility

The complete framework governing participation, trading, and conduct.

👥 Eligibility & Competition Tiers

  • The competition runs two parallel tracks for participants at different experience levels and from different backgrounds.
  • Gies Invitational: Open exclusively to currently enrolled UIUC students with prior coursework in finance, analytics, or investing — a more competitive, performance-focused environment.
  • A parallel track serves other experience levels; organizers publish full tier definitions before registration.
  • Teams of 2–4 members. All members must be currently enrolled UIUC students. Faculty and staff are not eligible to compete.
  • Each participant may belong to only one team. Register by the published deadline.

⏰ Trading Window & Orders

  • Only during the official competition period (dates TBC).
  • Trading hours: 8:30 AM–3:00 PM Central Time, Monday–Friday. No trading outside these hours or on market holidays. All timestamps are in Central Time; all values are in U.S. dollars.
  • Order types permitted: Market, limit, stop-loss, and bracket orders. Both long and short positions are allowed.
  • Margin: Not permitted unless explicitly supported and confirmed by IBKR demo account settings.
  • Minimum transactions per day: 0 (no forced daily trading). Max 25 trades per security per day. Organizers may set a weekly minimum activity threshold.

✅ Approved & Prohibited Assets

Platform: Interactive Brokers (IBKR) paper trading only. Starting capital: $100,000 virtual USD per team — equal for all.

Approved:

U.S. Equities (NYSE/NASDAQ/AMEX) ETFs Futures (if IBKR supports) Equity Options (standard cycles) Derivatives (if organizers enable)

Prohibited:

Cryptocurrencies & digital assets Bonds & fixed income Forex & currency trading

📐 Position Limits

  • Max 25% of starting capital ($25,000) in any single security at any one time.
  • Total options exposure may not exceed 50% of the portfolio at any one time.
  • Max 25 trades per single security per day.

🤖 Algorithmic Trading Policy

  • Strictly prohibited: Bots, scripts, macros, or any automated trading programs; algorithmic microtransaction strategies designed to exploit small price movements at high frequency; any automation that executes trades without direct human decision-making per transaction.
  • Permitted: AI tools used for research, screening, and analysis (e.g., ChatGPT for summarizing news, AI stock screeners) — educational use that does not automate execution.
  • Enforcement: Accounts suspected of algorithmic activity are flagged by the Tech Lead for review. Confirmed violations result in immediate disqualification.

Participant Guide

How it Works

Five steps from registration to the awards ceremony.

1

Register your team

Form a team of 2–4 currently enrolled UIUC students and register by the published deadline. Each student may only be on one team. All members must sign the Rule Acknowledgement before trading begins.

2

Onboard & Sandbox

Receive IBKR credentials and explore the platform during a 3–5 day practice window. Attend the mandatory Rules & Ethics webinar before live trading begins.

3

Trade the live window

Execute your strategy during official trading hours (8:30 AM–3:00 PM CT, Mon–Fri). No trading on market holidays or outside those hours. Human-directed trades only — bots and automation are strictly prohibited.

4

Document your work

Submit 5 required trade memos (2–3 sentences each: investment thesis, financial resource consulted, expected outcome) through the official platform or form. At competition close, submit a 1–2 page strategy summary or short presentation.

5

Judging & Awards

Accounts are frozen; top portfolios are audited; judges review all rationale submissions; a final audit report is produced. An awards ceremony with IBKR networking, a post-event survey, and an operations retrospective close out the program.

Evaluation

Scoring Methodology

Scoring is split equally between financial performance and quality of reasoning, reflecting the educational mission.

Score Breakdown

Portfolio Performance50%
Strategy & Rationale50%

Rankings are determined by P&L and key risk-adjusted metrics. Judges evaluate consistency of strategy and quality of reasoning.

📊 Portfolio Performance — 50%

  • Final account balance and P&L.
  • Key risk-adjusted performance metrics.
  • Rankings determined by P&L and risk-adjusted metrics.
  • Position-limit violations trigger a −5% deduction from the total P&L score per violation.

📝 Strategy & Rationale — 50%

  • Trade memos, final presentation or summary, and quality of research cited — evaluated by a panel of judges.
  • Judges evaluate consistency of strategy and quality of reasoning.

📌 Trade Memo Requirements

  • Five trades must be accompanied by a brief memo (2–3 sentences) submitted through the official platform or form.
  • Each memo must state: (1) the investment thesis, (2) the financial resource(s) consulted, and (3) the expected outcome.

📄 Final Strategy Summary

  • At competition close, teams must submit a 1–2 page strategy summary or short presentation outlining their overall approach.
  • Reviewed by the judges panel as part of the 50% rationale score.

Integrity Framework

Conduct & Governance

The standards and structures that protect the competition's integrity and fairness.

⚖️ Code of Conduct

  • Insider trading — zero tolerance: Immediate disqualification; matter may be referred to university administration or other authorities as appropriate.
  • Collusion & manipulation: Teams may not coordinate trades with other teams to manipulate leaderboard standings.
  • Account security: Teams may not share login credentials with anyone outside the team. Each team is responsible for all activity on its account.
  • Academic integrity: All trades must be executed by registered team members only. Participants must acknowledge the rulebook in writing before the competition begins. Any violation of university academic integrity policies is also a violation of this framework.
  • Respect: Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior may result in disqualification. Organizers apply rules consistently and without favoritism.

📢 Reporting

  • Suspicious activity must be reported immediately to the Operations Lead or via the official support channel.
  • Reports are reviewed by the Steering Committee within 48 hours.
🏛️

Governance Structure

  • Steering Committee: Holds final decision-making authority on the rulebook, dispute resolution, and long-term objectives. Includes the Student President, IBKR Sponsor Representative, and Faculty Lead / University Representative.
  • Operations Lead: Drives the team, manages timelines, assigns tasks, ensures deadlines are met.
  • Tech Lead: Sets up and monitors the IBKR environment, manages data feeds and leaderboards, handles technical issues.
  • Marketing Lead: Recruitment, social media, info sessions, and participant communications.
  • Sponsor Lead: Primary contact between IBKR and the operations team; may also serve as Finance Lead.
  • Judges: Define grading criteria, evaluate trade rationales, certify the final leaderboard.
  • University IT: Assists with platform access and security.
  • Faculty Advisors: Ensure alignment with academic integrity standards.
🔍

Dispute Resolution

  1. Participant raises the issue via the official Discord/Slack support channel or by emailing the Operations Lead.
  2. Operations Lead acknowledges within 24 hours and investigates.
  3. If resolved at the operations level, the decision is documented and communicated.
  4. If unresolved, the issue is escalated to the Steering Committee within 48 hours.
  5. The Steering Committee issues a final binding ruling. No further appeals are permitted within the competition.

📜 Amendments & Interpretation

  • Proposed changes must be submitted to the Operations Lead in writing.
  • Amendments require approval from the Steering Committee and must be made before the Rule Freeze date.
  • After the Rule Freeze, no changes to the rulebook are permitted for the duration of that competition cycle.
  • The Steering Committee holds final interpretive authority over all rules. In cases of ambiguity, its ruling is binding. Rulings are documented and shared with participants.
Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the competition.

Can I use AI tools during the competition?
Yes for research, screening, and analysis — for example summarizing news or using screeners. Automated trading, bots, macros, or anything that executes without direct human decision-making per trade is strictly prohibited and may lead to disqualification.
How big can my team be?
Teams must have 2–4 members. Every member must be a currently enrolled UIUC student, and each person may only compete on one team.
What can we trade?
U.S. equities listed on NYSE, NASDAQ, or AMEX; ETFs; and — when supported and enabled — futures and listed equity options. Derivatives are allowed only if IBKR supports them and organizers enable them. Cryptocurrencies, bonds, and forex are prohibited. Margin is not allowed unless explicitly supported in the IBKR demo configuration.
When can we place trades?
Only during the official competition period (dates to be confirmed), Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM–3:00 PM Central Time. No trading on market holidays or outside those hours.
How does scoring work?
Scoring is split 50% / 50%: portfolio performance (final balance, P&L, and risk-adjusted metrics) and strategy & rationale (trade memos, final presentation or summary, and research quality, judged by a panel). Position-limit violations reduce the P&L score component; code-of-conduct issues may be penalized or disqualified at judges' discretion; confirmed algorithmic trading or insider trading results in disqualification.
How do I report a problem or dispute?
Use the official Discord/Slack support channel or email the Operations Lead. The Operations Lead responds within 24 hours; unresolved matters can be escalated to the Steering Committee, whose decision is final for the competition.
What does the TRACE chatbot do?
TRACE helps you navigate this site and answer common questions. Binding interpretations, disputes, and formal rulings follow the Operations Lead and Steering Committee process described on this page.
